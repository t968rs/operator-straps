# bubble-stamp shows the elapsed time

Date: 2026-11-20

## Request

Each bubble-stamp bubble should show how long its turn took, so a slow turn stands out at a glance.

## Goals

- Each bubble shows the elapsed time of its turn.
- The elapsed time is shown in whole minutes.
- The stamp sits at the right edge of the bubble.

## Avoid

- The stamp never covers the bubble text.
